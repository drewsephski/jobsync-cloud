## Platform

## Users

People managing an active job search are the inferred primary users, based on the hosted product's current workflow. Further audience details are open.

## Product Purpose

JobSync Cloud is a hosted workspace that brings resume preparation, role and company discovery, and application tracking together. The core journey moves from account setup and resume review to relevant openings and application tracking.

## Positioning

The current product promise is a focused job-search workspace with resume, opportunity, and next-step details together. A more distinct market position is not established in the available product material.

## Operating Context

Users review AI-proposed resume details and edits, choose target roles, explore job matches, track applications, and manage billing. Consequential resume edits remain user-approved.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Resume upload, structured review, editing, and confirmation.
- Target-role preferences, company watches, and job discovery.
- Application records, status history, and follow-up tracking.
- AI assistance is bounded by account entitlements and platform safeguards.
- Current offer: a 14-day free trial without a card, followed by an optional Plus subscription listed at US$6 per month.
- Do not imply autonomous applications or outreach, guaranteed job availability, invented qualifications, unlimited AI, or fabricated search results.
- No verified unique market differentiator is recorded yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name in current app: JobSync Cloud; paid plan: JobSync Plus.
- The user requests a refined, minimal workspace with soft neutral backgrounds, restrained blue accents, thin borders, subtle depth, and a mobile bottom dock.

- Keep the user's job-search information together and in their control.
- Treat AI output as a proposal that the user reviews.
- Preserve real records and clearly identify illustrative material.
- Keep applications and next steps easy to follow.
